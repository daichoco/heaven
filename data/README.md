# Data files

Tabular datasets use JSON Lines (`.jsonl`): one JSON object per record. Read and write them with `src/data_store.py`, which validates expected columns and replaces files atomically.

- `tokai_fuzoku_reports.jsonl`: collected Tokai Fuzoku articles. Generated by `src/tokai_fuzoku/collect_fuzoku_tokai.py` and read by `src/tokai_fuzoku/generate_report_final.py`.
- The Tokai Fuzoku collector uses the newest `date` in this file as its cutoff and only processes articles newer than that date.
- `tokai_fuzoku_report_history.jsonl`: extracted report history used to match reports with CityHeaven profiles. Updated by `src/integrations/report_left_merge.py`. `evaluations` is a JSON object.
- `okinilove_reviews.jsonl`: Okinilove review records, maintained by `src/okinilove/okinilove_left_merge.py`; includes `face`, `service`, and `age` details when available, with face/service supplemented from `okinilove_report_details.json`.
- `okinilove_shop_index.json`: cached shop index (shop name -> 全投稿履歴 URL) built from Okinilove category pages (categories 70–72: デリヘル・ヘルス・ソープランド); refreshed every 7 days. Review scraping excludes shops identified as ぽっちゃり系 by their name or category industry label, and category 70 also excludes shops with age-related terms in the name or category-70 industry label. The generated people-review HTML hides ぽっちゃり系 shops by default and provides a checkbox to show them.
  `report_left_merge.py` only re-links rows with an empty `cityheaven_url` by matching shop + name against people on the CityHeaven calendar (no network). The slow maintenance steps are off by default and run separately with `python okinilove/maintain_okinilove.py --check-unavailable` (HTTP 404/410 removal check) and/or `--enrich [--time-budget SECONDS]` (shop-page search via `src/ns_senmon/match_cityheaven.py`, prioritising shops on the calendar), or inside `report_left_merge.py` with `OKINILOVE_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE=1` / `OKINILOVE_SEARCH_ENRICH=1`.
- `okinilove_cityheaven_enrich_cache.json`: `"shop_name\tgirl_name"` -> `{checked_at, url}` for the search-based re-linking above; unresolved pairs are retried after 7 days. Committed by the scrape workflow so the retry window survives between runs.
- `okinilove_profile_status.json`: generated 7-day profile status cache; excluded from Git. Cached results are reused until they expire, independently of new-review collection; timeouts and connection failures are not cached and are retried on the next run. When the removal check runs, people shown on the CityHeaven calendar (`data/cityheaven_profiles.json`, if present) are treated as still listed and are not checked.
- `ns_senmon_profile_image_cache.json`: `cityheaven_url` -> `{checked_at, status, image_url, shop_checked, shop_kind, price_class}` for the CityHeaven profile photos and shop type shown in `docs/ns_senmon_report.html`. `shop_kind` is the text after the shop name in the profile page's `.shopname` (e.g. `ソープ(高級/金津園)`) and `price_class` is its price tier (`高級` / `スタンダード` / `大衆` / ... or empty); the page's 店舗種別 filter treats `高級` as luxury and everything else as 高級以外, while people without these keys (not linked or not fetched) appear only under すべて. Written by `src/ns_senmon/profile_images.py` when `src/ns_senmon/generate_ns_senmon_report.py` runs. `ok` entries are refreshed after 90 days (entries without `shop_checked` are re-fetched immediately), `no_image` / `error` are retried after 7 days, and `not_found` (HTTP 404/410) is never re-fetched. Fetching is capped at 120 seconds per run; failures leave the person without a photo. Commit it together with the regenerated HTML.

Published pages in `docs/` share `docs/common.css` (static, edited by hand; dark mode and iPhone/safe-area rules) and `src/web_common.py` (shared head, 「← 一覧」 nav, 「先頭へ」 button). `src/okinilove/render_people_summary.py` writes the review bodies (face/style/service/text) to `docs/okinilove_review_bodies.json`, which the people-review page fetches when a person is opened; review links, ratings, and gaps stay in the HTML because the scrapers re-parse it.
People-review positive/negative rates count reviews containing at least one corresponding face, photo-gap, or style item, divided by reviews containing a recognized positive, negative, or neutral item. Mixed reviews count toward both rates; unknown/unclassifiable-only reviews are excluded. Counts for positive-only, mixed, negative-only, and neutral-only reviews are displayed. Photo matches are positive, youthful appearance is neutral, and style `ナイスボディ` is positive. `ぽっちゃり` and `おなかぽっこり` (including `お腹` / `ポッコリ` spelling variants) are negative according to the selected preferences, not universal judgments; `デブ` and `ちょいぽちゃ` remain neutral pending explicit classification changes. Unknown values, including misplaced face `ナイスボディ`, do not contribute to the denominator.
Service items also contribute to these review-level rates and the recommendation score. Positive service items are `フェラ上手`, `キス上手`, `一生懸命`, `テクニシャン`, `手コキ上手`, and `いちゃいちゃ`; negative items are `事務的`, `サバサバ`, `手抜き`, and `地雷`. Other recognized service items, including `ぎこちない`, are neutral. Service-only reviews with recognized items are eligible; multiple positive or negative items still count only once per review. SP service filtering accepts both `SP` and `SP（スペシャルサービス）`.
Default recommendation sorting compares corrected average stars rounded to one decimal (descending), corrected negative rate (ascending), corrected positive rate (descending), then eligible review count (descending). Stars are corrected by adding three reviews at the global star-rated-review-weighted average; rates are corrected with three reviews at the corresponding global eligible-review-weighted rate. Global baselines include all people on the page and do not change with filters. People without star ratings sort last in recommendation mode; within a star bucket, people without eligible item evaluations follow those with evaluations. Raw positive/negative sorting uses eligible review count then stars and places people without eligible reviews last; average-star sorting uses star-rated count then eligible count. Count sorting uses eligible count then stars. The three-review correction and one-decimal buckets are heuristics, not calibrated confidence estimates. Style and service body entries use `[text, tone]` pairs; the page also accepts legacy strings.

Do not add derived join keys such as `url_key` to the persisted records. Compute them at runtime.
